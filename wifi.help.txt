Commands:
    
    on                  Turns on wifi
    off                 Turns off wifi
    list                Lists all available networks
    status              Current status of wifi
    connect <ifname>    Attemps to connect to network <ifname> and prompts for password
    deconnect <ifname>  Deconnects computer from inputted network
    delete <ifname>     Deletes connection information of inputted network
